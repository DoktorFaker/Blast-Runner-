# Neon Blast Arena – CrazyGames upload guide

## Files
- **Game files:** upload `Neon_Blast_Arena_CrazyGames.zip` as it is (do not unzip). `index.html` is at the top of the zip, next to `audio/` and `fonts/`.
- **Engine:** HTML5 · **SDK:** HTML5 SDK v3 (built in) · **Orientation:** landscape and portrait · **Platforms:** desktop and mobile

## Store text

**Title:** Neon Blast Arena

**Short description:**
Survive endless waves of creatures warping into neon arenas. Blast them with bombs, bazookas and grenades, chain combos, and pick a new upgrade after every wave!

**Description:**
Neon Blast Arena is an explosive arena survival game with a roguelite twist.

Creatures warp in through glowing portals, wave after wave. Drop bombs, slide them into crowds, fire your bazooka, lob mortars and throw grenades to wipe out every wave. Quick kills chain into a combo multiplier, and every creature drops energy crystals that charge your NOVA: an arena-wide shockwave that clears the screen in slow motion.

After every wave you choose 1 of 3 upgrade cards: Chain Reaction, Napalm, Energy Shield, Crystal Magnet, Quick Fuse, Extra Bomb and more. Every run builds differently. How many waves can you survive?

**Features**
- Endless waves across 20 different cyberpunk arenas, with boss waves every 10th wave
- Pick 1 of 3 upgrade cards after every wave: 14 upgrades to combine
- Combo multiplier up to ×5 and the arena-clearing NOVA overdrive
- Bombs, Mortar, Bazooka and Grenades from the start, plus 4 unlockable special weapons: Lightning, Freeze, Laser and Airstrike
- 15 kinds of creatures, from drones and mech spiders to slimes that split in two
- 14 robot skins (including secret ones) and 7 dance moves to unlock
- Hall of Fame, records and detailed statistics
- Original soundtrack
- Plays on desktop, mobile and with a controller

**Controls**
- Move: Arrow keys / WASD (ZQSD on AZERTY)
- Drop bomb: Space (drop while moving to slide it)
- Mortar: C · Bazooka: V · Grenade: G
- NOVA (when charged): R
- Special weapon: F · Switch special weapon: Q
- Detonate (with the Detonator upgrade): X
- Pick an upgrade card: 1 / 2 / 3 or click
- Dance: E · Pause: P
- Mobile: on-screen D-pad (or drag on the playfield) and weapon buttons
- Controller: A bomb · B grenade · X bazooka · Y mortar · RT NOVA · RB special · LB detonate · LT switch weapon · View dance · Menu pause

## What's built in for CrazyGames
- Lands directly in gameplay, with a skippable visual controls card for new players
- SDK v3: gameplayStart / gameplayStop, happytime on wave clear
- Midgame ad after you pick an upgrade between waves (CrazyGames controls the pacing)
- Rewarded ad on game over: "Watch ad: continue with 2 lives" (once per game)
- Game and sound pause during ads; respects the CrazyGames mute setting
- CrazyGames username used as player name; progress saved with the SDK data module
- Works with adblock / without the SDK
- No external links, no fullscreen button, no Escape key binding
